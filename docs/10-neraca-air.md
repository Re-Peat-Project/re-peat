# 10 Neraca air: the physical water balance of a KHG

Moved to the `repeat-neraca` repository (2026-10-20): `docs/neraca-air.md` there describes the model (KHG-level and 2D groundwater, no fitted parameters), how to run it, the first results on Sungai Sugihan-Sungai Lumpur and Sungai Kampar-Sungai Gaung, and what is still missing. The reports quoted in it are in that repository's `docs/reports/`. The earlier bucket-model experiments (fitted to stations, Sentinel-1, finer forcing, canal level) and their reports are kept there too.

What neraca hands to the other modules is in [11 Data contracts](11-data-contracts.md), section C5.
