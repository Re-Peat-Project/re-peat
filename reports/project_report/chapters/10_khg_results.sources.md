# Sources, chapter 10

Tables 10.1 to 10.3 are generated by `re-peat/tools/project_report_compare.py` (`khg_readiness`, `khg_completed_lidar`, `khg_completed_open`; `khg_completed` holds both) at build time. The summary table of chapter 1 is `summary_key_figures` from the same file.

- Table 10.1: national profile `re-peat/reports/national_profile/operational/profile.csv` (nama, province, peringkat, kelas, peat_km2, canal_km, n_infra_ada, n_tma_stations); LiDAR and modelled peat-depth coverage of the KHG polygon from `re-peat/sites/khg_data_recap.csv` (theme lidar_dtm tag LiDAR; theme peat_depth tag model; coverage_pct); for Kampar-Gaung, which is not in the recap, LiDAR cover from `re-peat/sites/khg_dem_inputs.csv` (product lidar_*, cover_pct). Full chain: the DEM variants with `05_pump/<dem>/report.html` and layout PNGs in each `_khg/<folder>`. Printed 30 October 2026: 14 KHG; 11 of class high (ranks 1 to 138) and Kampar-Gaung 178, Pulau Padang 194, Pulau Tebing Tinggi 204 (medium).
- Tables 10.2 and 10.3: `_khg/<folder>/02_delineation/<dem>/delineation_report.json` (n_subkhg); `03_neraca/runs/deficit/neraca_khg.csv` (deficit_mm, deficit_m3, n_stations; a KHG without the file shows "no stations"); `04_prioritisation/<dem>/ringkasan_kelas.csv` (class tinggi: unit, luas_km2); `04_prioritisation/<dem>/kebutuhan_sekat.csv` (n_sekat_perlu, n_sekat_ada, summed); `05_pump/<dem>/run_report.json` (sekat_balance.head_rule, sekat_balance.kept, budget.total_idr). 33 analyses: 5 KHG x 3 and 9 KHG x 2.
- KLH spacing (min_new_spacing_m 500, dedup_tol_m 100) in all 33 runs: `05_pump/<dem>/run_report.json` config.sekat. Pumping not enabled in any of the 33 analyses: stated by the project lead (30 October 2026).
- Ranges and factors in section "Across the KHG": computed from Tables 10.2 and 10.3 per KHG (maximum over minimum of blocks needed across models: 2.58, 5.68, 4.39, 6.65, 7.04 at the five LiDAR KHG; DEMNAS over DeltaDTM 1.63 Bengkalis, 2.28 Kahayan-Sebangau, 4.04 Mempawah-Peniti, 4.15 Buru Mappi-Buru Obaa; kept blocks DEMNAS over DeltaDTM 1.29 to 4.33 at the nine, 1.35 to 3.8 across models at the five; high-need area 1.16 to 1.39 across models at the five, 1.01 to 1.23 DeltaDTM against DEMNAS at the nine; budget DEMNAS against DeltaDTM 1.0 to 5.7).
- Rain over evapotranspiration at the three Riau KHG with 0 mm deficit (153, 220, 105 mm; 1 June to 15 October): `03_neraca/runs/deficit/neraca_khg.csv`, column net_balance_mm (ERA5 P - E), Pulau Padang, Pulau Bengkalis, Pulau Tebing Tinggi. Station counts of the deficit (78 of 92 at Kampar-Gaung, 3 of 5 at Pungurbesar-Kapuas): column n_stations against n_tma_stations of the national profile.
- Budget items: `05_pump/<dem>/budget.csv`, column cost_idr summed by item over the 33 runs. Kept blocks (sekat_standard) 0.09 (Air Hitam Laut-Buntu Kecil, DEMNAS) to 2.11 billion Rp (Mempawah-Peniti, DeltaDTM). Largest item per run: revegetation_ha in 14 runs, sekat_ipg_estimate in 19. Pungurbesar-Kapuas DEMNAS: sekat_ipg_estimate 192.37 of 193.4; Mempawah-Peniti DEMNAS: 156.53 of 157.6.
- Canal density of Mempawah-Peniti (4,915 km on 376 km² of peat, 13 km per km²) and Buru Mappi-Buru Obaa (31 km on 1,699 km²): Table 10.1.
- Block spacing mechanism (0.4 m divided by canal slope; median canal slope eight times the LiDAR slope on DEMNAS at Sugihan-Lumpur): chapter 7, section 7.5.
- Existing blocks: Table 10.2 and 10.3 column Blocks existing. - Pulau Padang 2 m LiDAR left out of the chain by decision: chapter 3, section 3.7.
- Saleh-Sugihan LiDAR about 62 % of the KHG: chapter 4, section 4.9; existing blocks 0 on the LiDAR variant: Table 10.2.
- Main KHG, reserves and the open Cawang-Lalang point: `re-peat/docs/13-selected-khg.md`, `re-peat/sites/selected_khg.csv`; chapter 2.
