## Discrepancies between docs and code, and other contradictions

1. Need-score weight of the balance (corrected): `repeat-neraca/docs/neraca-air-method.md` says the need score gives the balance weight 0, and the code agrees. The general default in `repeat-prioritisation/src/repeat_prioritisation/common/scoring.py` lines 15-16 is 1.0, but the sub-KHG ranking overrides it: `repeat-prioritisation/src/repeat_prioritisation/subkhg/prioritise.py` lines 45-50 set `u_wb_mean`, `u_deficit_months` and the other climate indicators to weight 0.0. No discrepancy.
2. Stability threshold: the method report says "above" the 99th percentile; `balance.py` passes at greater than or equal (`med >= q_med`, `r >= q_one`). The chapter says "at or above".
3. Deficit band: `repeat-neraca/docs/neraca-air.md` gives SL 290 mm (227-296) and KG 7 mm (0-12), which include the WaPOR bound. The code default is `wapor_bound: False` (`deficit.py`), and `neraca_khg.csv` gives 284.7-295.7 and 1.6-12.5. The chapter uses the CSV values. `neraca-air-method.md` gives 285-296 and 2-12, consistent with the CSV.
4. `neraca-air.md` still says WaPOR "gives the other bound" of the deficit; the code has it off by default.
5. Version: `neraca-air-method.md` and the diagram say repeat-neraca 0.1.0; `repeat-neraca/README.md` says 0.2.0.
6. Method report decision 1 and section 8 V5 give a Spearman of 0.10 between the per-unit station deficit and P - ET at SL. No such value appears in `unit_deficit_v5.md` or `balance_design.md`; the chapter does not use it.
7. Stability at SL: the method report gives the flag as 1 (30-unit set). The chain's DEM-variant runs give 0 (LiDAR, DeltaDTM) and 1 (DEMNAS); `_khg/neraca_balance_summary.csv`. Reported in the chapter.
8. `_khg/pipeline_status.csv` marks the Saleh-Sugihan deficit step "done", but the log shows a failed run with 0 stations and no deficit file exists.
9. Reproduction against the 2021 report: the scripts are reproduced within 0.1 mm, but the published Table 5.10 range (41-65 mm/month, 3.9-5.3 months, `references.md`) is matched by neither C = 0.25 (49.3-54.0) nor the split C (28.7-50.9).
10. Station change at SL: `neraca-air.md` says the water table "fell 14 cm" (monthly means June to October), `neraca_khg.csv` gives +11.0 cm (first and last 7 days of the deficit window). The chapter uses 11 cm.
11. `neraca_khg.csv` for Kampar-Gaung carries `khg_id` 71 instead of the registry code 14.02-04-05.01 (not used in the text).
12. The KHG mean balance in `_khg/neraca_balance_summary.csv` and the calendar in each `neraca_balance.md` are unweighted means over sub-KHG (`batch.py` `t["u_wb_mean"].mean()`; `balance.py` `wbs[central].mean(axis=1)`), whereas KHG-mean rain inside the balance is area weighted. The chapter labels them as unweighted.
13. AGENTS.md says Mendahara-Batanghari has 6 community stations; these belong to the groundwater model's station export. The adopted deficit uses the 12 concession stations of `03_neraca/inputs/stations.csv`.
14. The bucket experiments (section 8.3) cannot be checked against code: the package was removed on 2026-10-26.
