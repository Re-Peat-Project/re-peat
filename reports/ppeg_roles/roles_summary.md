# Roles in the KLH priority area

KLH's priority area is the peat with damaged ecosystem status (SKEG 2024), 3.31 million ha in 362 priority KHG. KLH's integrated thematic analysis for peat ecosystem restoration (PPEG, April 2026) assigns every part of it to one actor. The roles below are those stated for the FGD of 2 November 2026; the areas are what each actor would cover.

## Roles as stated

| Institution | Role | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Ministry of Public Works (PU) | Technical support, field implementation and coordination of canal blocks on primary and secondary canals | FGD terms of reference, agenda item 9 |
| Ministry of Forestry | Technical support, water management and rewetting infrastructure in the forest area, with and without licences | FGD terms of reference, agenda item 9 |
| BPGM (national peat and mangrove agency) | Aligning peat targets and priorities; coordination and synergy. Coordinates the ministries and does not replace them | FGD terms of reference, agenda item 9; FGD notes (Head of BPGMN) |
| Bappenas | Direction of support and budget for peat restoration; financing and planning | FGD terms of reference, agenda item 9; FGD notes (Head of BPGMN) |
| KLH/BPLH | Sets the priority area (SKEG) and the PPEG analysis; assigned rewetting in APL outside licences | PPEG analysis; FGD terms of reference |
| Licence holders | Water management and rewetting inside their licences; water sharing with communities around them | PPEG analysis; FGD notes (KLH Deputy, UNRI) |
| Ministry of Finance | No role stated in the FGD documents | - |

## By institution

- **Ministry of Public Works.** 1,166 thousand ha of the priority area lies in the zone affected by primary or secondary canals, the canals the terms of reference assign to PU. The priority KHG hold 25,391 km of primary and 73,150 km of secondary canals in the national canal layer (233,497 km tertiary); 44 of them have no canal in that layer, where canals may exist unmapped, so these are lower bounds.
- **Ministry of Forestry.** 436 thousand ha outside licences in the forest area (13% of the priority area). With the licensed forest area its stated role covers, 1,795 thousand ha (54%) lies in the forest area, 898 thousand ha of it inside licences.
- **KLH/BPLH.** 298 thousand ha in APL outside licences (9%).
- **Licence holders.** 1,589 thousand ha inside licences (48%) and 989 thousand ha in the 5 km buffer (30%), where KLH foresees collaboration with communities.
- **BPGM and Bappenas.** Plan and fund the whole: 3,070 thousand ha to rewet and 783 thousand ha to revegetate across 362 KHG. An indicative investment envelope needs the block and well quantities per KHG; it is the next step of the prioritisation.
