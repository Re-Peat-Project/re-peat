# 2 The 2026 fire season

VIIRS gives the 2026 figures in this report. It sees fires at 375 m against 1 km for MODIS, and one sensor (S-NPP) has recorded Indonesia since 2012, so 2026 can be set against earlier years over the same months. MODIS is the series KLH reports; its 2026 total is given beside each VIIRS figure where it matters. The 2026 VIIRS record runs to 31 October 2026: January to July from the standard product, August to October from the near-real-time product. November and December are not yet in it.

![VIIRS hotspots per month in 2026 against the mean of 2015 to 2025. Medium and high confidence, likely gas flares and volcanoes left out. Source: NASA FIRMS VIIRS S-NPP.](re-peat/reports/fire_landstatus/figures/fig_months_2026.png)

Hotspots stayed near the usual level until June. From July they rose far above it: 198,786 in September, against a mean of 62,092 for that month in 2015 to 2025. Over 1 January to 31 October 2026, VIIRS counts 572,701 hotspots, 3.2 times the mean of 2015 to 2025; only 2015 had more over the same months.

![VIIRS hotspots per year from 1 January to 31 October, inside and outside concessions. Source: NASA FIRMS VIIRS S-NPP, licences 2025.](re-peat/reports/fire_landstatus/figures/fig_years.png)

![VIIRS hotspots of 2026 per 100 km² of land, by province. Source: NASA FIRMS VIIRS S-NPP, village boundaries 2023.](re-peat/reports/fire_landstatus/figures/fig_map_2026.png)

Five provinces hold 69% of the 2026 hotspots: Kalimantan Tengah (164,968), Kalimantan Barat (79,375), Papua Selatan (72,322), Sumatera Selatan (49,779), Kalimantan Timur (30,175). The share inside concessions differs widely between them, from 16% to 64%.

![The ten provinces with the most VIIRS hotspots in 2026, inside and outside concessions. Source: NASA FIRMS VIIRS S-NPP, licences 2025.](re-peat/reports/fire_landstatus/figures/fig_provinces.png)

![VIIRS hotspots of 2026 per 100 km² by land status; dashed line: all land. Source: this analysis.](re-peat/reports/fire_landstatus/figures/fig_rates.png)

Near known static heat sources such as gas flares, 11,449 further VIIRS detections are set apart. With them the 2026 total is 584,150, and the shares by land status change by at most 0.7 percentage points. With them, MODIS counts 220,060.
