# 9 DMPG villages

DMPG villages cover 8.7 million ha, 5% of the land, and hold 25% of the burned area of 2015 to 2025 and 27% of the 2026 VIIRS hotspots. The programme chose villages on fire-prone peat, so the comparison below keeps to peat in the 10 provinces that hold DMPG villages.

![VIIRS hotspots of 2026 per 100 km² of peat in DMPG villages and on other peat, by province (provinces with at least 100 km² of peat on each side). Source: NASA FIRMS VIIRS S-NPP, SiTMAT DMPG villages, KLH peat (PPEG 2026).](re-peat/reports/fire_landstatus/figures/fig_dmpg.png)

On this comparable land, 18% of the DMPG village peat burned at least once in 2015 to 2026, against 7% of other peat. VIIRS hotspots of 2026 were 3.7 times the 2015 to 2025 mean in DMPG villages and 3.4 times elsewhere. The project's investigation of the 2026 season compared the rise in hotspots on DMPG village land with the rise on land outside it within the same KHG and land tenure class. The ratio of the two rises was 0.87 (95% interval 0.60 to 1.14, 28 KHG), so that test was inconclusive: it neither shows nor rules out a smaller rise in DMPG villages. Table A.7 gives the values.
