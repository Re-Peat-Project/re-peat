# 9 DMPG villages

DMPG villages cover 8.7 million ha, 5% of the land, and hold 25% of the burned area of 2015 to 2025 and 27% of the 2026 VIIRS hotspots. The programme chose villages on fire-prone peat, so a national comparison mixes the programme with where it works. Table 9.1 compares DMPG village peat with other peat in the 10 provinces that hold DMPG villages.

On this comparable land, 18% of the DMPG village peat burned at least once in 2015 to 2026, against 7% of other peat. VIIRS hotspots of 2026 were 3.7 times the 2015 to 2025 mean in DMPG villages and 3.6 times elsewhere. The project's investigation of the 2026 season compared the rise in hotspots on DMPG village land with the rise on land outside it within the same KHG and land tenure class. The ratio of the two rises was 0.87 (95% interval 0.60 to 1.14, 28 KHG), so that test was inconclusive: it neither shows nor rules out a smaller rise in DMPG villages.
