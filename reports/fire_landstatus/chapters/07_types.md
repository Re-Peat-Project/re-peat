![VIIRS hotspots of 2026 by concession type: number and share (left) and the forest area function of the hotspots inside each type (right). Source: this analysis.](re-peat/reports/fire_landstatus/figures/fig_types.png)

Most 2026 hotspots lie outside any concession (74%). Of those inside one, most are in forest concessions (PBPH, 17% of all hotspots) and oil palm (6%).
