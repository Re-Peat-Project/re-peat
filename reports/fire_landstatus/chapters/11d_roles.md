# 15 Roles

The terms of reference of the focus group discussion of 2 November 2026 set out what KLH asks of each ministry; the discussion notes add the roles of BPGM and Bappenas. Table 15.1 sets these roles beside the part of the priority area each covers.

Table: Roles as stated for the focus group discussion and the part of the priority area each covers. Source: FGD terms of reference (KLH/BPLH, letter B.70/E/PKL.4.5/10/2026, agenda item 9), FGD notes of 2 November 2026, KLH PPEG integrated analysis.

| Institution | Role | Part of the priority area |
|---|---|---|
| Ministry of Public Works | Technical support, field implementation and coordination of canal blocks on primary and secondary canals | 1.2 million ha in the zone affected by primary or secondary canals; 25,391 km of primary and 73,150 km of secondary canals in the priority KHG |
| Ministry of Forestry | Technical support, water management and rewetting infrastructure in the forest area, with and without licences | 436 thousand ha outside licences; 1.8 million ha in the forest area in all, 898 thousand ha of it inside licences |
| KLH/BPLH | Sets the priority area and the PPEG analysis; rewetting in APL outside licences | 298 thousand ha |
| Licence holders | Water management inside their licences; water sharing with the communities around them | 1.6 million ha inside licences, 989 thousand ha in the buffer |
| BPGM | Aligns peat targets and priorities; coordinates the ministries without replacing them | 362 priority KHG |
| Bappenas | Direction of support and budget for peat restoration; planning and financing | 3.1 million ha to rewet, 783 thousand ha to revegetate |
| Ministry of Finance | No role stated in the FGD documents | |

The canal lengths come from the national canal layer, which is drawn from OpenStreetMap; 44 priority KHG have no canal in it, so the lengths are lower bounds. An indicative investment envelope needs the number of canal blocks and wells per KHG. It follows from the prioritisation of the KHG within the priority area.
