# 5 Completeness of the burned-area maps

Burned area and hotspots measure the same fires in different ways. Between 2015 and 2025 the burned area per VIIRS hotspot lies between 2.1 and 6.4 ha in every year except 2024 (0.57). In 2026 it is 0.20: VIIRS recorded 572,701 hotspots on land to 31 October, the second highest since 2012, after 2015 (765,124), while the burned-area map holds 111,781 ha. In the provinces with the most hotspots in 2026 the ratio is Kalimantan Tengah 0.02, Kalimantan Barat 0.36, Papua Selatan 0.09. The 2026 map, and that of 2024, are therefore incomplete as delivered.
