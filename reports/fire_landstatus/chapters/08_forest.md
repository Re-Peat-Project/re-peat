# 8 Forest area function

![Burned area 2015 to 2025 (sum of the yearly areas, so land that burned twice counts twice) flowing from forest area function to concession type to peat. Source: KLHK burned area, licences 2025, forest area 2023, KLH peat (PPEG 2026).](re-peat/reports/fire_landstatus/figures/fig_alluvial.png)

Of the area burned in 2015 to 2025, 44% lay in the forest area outside any concession and 36% in other land use (APL) outside any concession. Among forest functions, convertible production forest (HPK) had the densest 2026 hotspots (63 per 100 km²) and protection forest the sparsest (19). Tables A.5 and B.6 give the values.
