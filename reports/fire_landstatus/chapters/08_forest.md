# 8 Forest area function

![Burned area 2015 to 2025 (sum of the yearly areas, so land that burned twice counts twice) flowing from forest area function to concession type to peat. Source: KLHK burned area, licences 2025, forest area 2023, KLH peat (PPEG 2026).](re-peat/reports/fire_landstatus/figures/fig_alluvial.png)

Of the area burned in 2015 to 2025, 44% lay in the forest area outside any concession and 36% in other land use (APL) outside any concession. Of the 2026 hotspots, 31% lay in production forest, 12% in convertible production forest (HPK), 12% in conservation areas, 10% in protection forest and 35% in other land use (APL). Tables A.5 and B.6 give the values.
