# 10 Repeatedly burned land

Of the 189.1 million ha of land, 5.6 million ha burned at least once in 2015 to 2026 and 1.5 million ha burned in two or more different years: 997 thousand ha twice, 315 thousand ha three times and 177 thousand ha four times or more. A cell counts as burned twice when the burned-area maps of two different years cover it. The maps of 2024 and 2026 are incomplete (chapter 5), so these counts are lower bounds.

Most repeatedly burned land lies outside concessions: 46% in the forest area and 39% in other land use (APL) without a licence. Concessions hold 22% of the land, 23% of the land ever burned and 15% of the land burned twice or more. Inside concessions, burned land burned again less often: 14% of the ever-burned land in the forest area and 24% in APL, against 28% and 31% outside them.

DMPG villages cover 5% of the land and hold 26% of the land ever burned and 26% of the land burned twice or more. 16.7% of their land burned at least once, against 2.3% elsewhere; the programme chose villages on fire-prone peat (chapter 9). Within burned land the share that burned again is about the same in and outside DMPG villages (27% and 26%). Table A.11 gives the values.
