# 11 Peat

Peat is taken from the composite peat map of Global Forest Watch. It was obtained for the map tiles that touch a KHG. The other 141.1 million ha of land (none of it inside a KHG) has no peat information and counts as not peat, so the peat figures are a lower bound outside the KHG.
